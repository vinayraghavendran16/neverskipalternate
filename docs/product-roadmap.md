# Northstar product roadmap

## Delivered

- Phases 1–4: tenancy, people, academics, attendance, teacher daily work, homework and assessments.
- Phase 5: family learning, announcements, leave approvals, homework responses and fee visibility.
- Phase 6: school calendar, transport operations, attendance-correction review and guided assessment setup.

## Prioritized next

1. Production telemetry, operational health checks and administrator-visible failure reporting.
2. Consent-based email, SMS and app notification delivery with retries and delivery receipts.
3. Payment gateway orders, signed webhooks, idempotent settlement, reconciliation and refunds.
4. Offline attendance and teacher-work queues with conflict handling.
5. Admissions, document verification and enrollment conversion.

## Parked

The cross-platform “Ask Northstar” text and voice assistant, ElevenLabs integration and automated voice announcements are parked by product decision. They should not be implemented or enabled until the school-data privacy, retention, consent, model-provider and cost decisions are approved.
